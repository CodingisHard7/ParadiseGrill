Our little slice of ChampionsGate

Creating Paradise,
One Plate at a Time

Paradise Grille at Enclaves is a resort-style casual dining restaurant in the heart of ChampionsGate, Florida. Escape to the tropics without leaving the neighborhood — elevated comfort food, tropical-inspired cuisine, and a bar that takes its cocktails seriously.

Whether it's date night, a family celebration, or a casual dinner with friends, locals, families, and visitors all come together here for fresh flavors and unforgettable times.
